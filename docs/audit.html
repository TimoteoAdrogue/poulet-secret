Poulet Secret audit

I went through pouletsecret.ch on 3 November 2026 and listed every flaw I found. Each one has the options I weighed and the one I used (✓) in the redesign. I worked in your priority order: hero first, then motion, then how each section hands over to the next. All text is the client's own, word for word, from their site, their TV menu PDF and their opening-hours story on Linktree. The only change is one typo fix.

Live site: one HTML file of 1.5 MB on Netlify. Real photos used: their 10 (shop, festivals, Cheesy Bacon), logo traced to vector. AI images: none.

1 · The hero

01

Two headlines fight, and there is no H1

The tagline is set at 32 px in gold neon, right on top of the shop's own lit "FRIED CHICKEN" sign in the photo, so neither one reads. The page has no <h1>.

  1. Crop the photo so the sign clears the text
  2. Darken the photo and enlarge the tagline
  3. Separate them: the photo goes inside the logo, and "The Best fried chicken in Suisse" becomes the H1 at ~8 vw beside it, with "fried" outlined and "chicken" in brand red
02

First frame shows neither the brand nor the food

The hero is a dark night shot, 1600 px wide, stretched edge to edge. The logo is a 38 px icon in the nav.

  1. Cheesy Bacon photo full-bleed (only 900 px: soft on big screens)
  2. New photo shoot (best, but slow)
  3. The red chicken logo becomes a window onto the shop, outlined in red neon like their sign, so brand and place both land in the first second. Food comes one scroll later. Ask the client for the original photo files (B) for launch
03

The hero doesn't move and leads nowhere

The only motion is a looping neon flicker.

  1. Video background
  2. Parallax photo
  3. Scrolling walks you into the shop through the logo: it zooms through the chicken's deepest point, flies past the stencil letters, and the full "FRIED CHICKEN" storefront fills the screen. Then the tray paper slides over it and the next scene starts with no cut

2 · Motion and transitions

04

Nothing shows how the food is made

"Fait maison" is just a label.

  1. AI video of frying (tried: free plans watermark the output, and AI food fails the "you can tell" test)
  2. Stock footage (not their food)
  3. Their Secret du mois gets built step by step as you scroll, drawn in the red line style of their own wrapper paper: 01 poulet frit (into the oil, crust forms, lifted out) · 02 pain brioché · 03 sauce Poulet Secret ("C'est un secret, demande pas", with the words blacked out) · 04 bacon · 05 cornichons · 06 cheddar poured over · 07 bacon bits. Then the drawing turns into their real photo with the price. The steps are their ingredient list, word for word
05

Sections are stacked boxes with 1 px borders

There are no transitions. The only motion is a slider that auto-advances every 4 s.

  1. Fade-ins on scroll
  2. Generic parallax
  3. Every handover borrows a real Poulet Secret object. The tray paper slides over the shop. The checkered liner divides sections. The menu screens switch on like their TVs. The festival photos scroll sideways like a road trip, with the festival names in a ticker that speeds up as you scroll. The shop neon lights only when the shop is open

3 · What the visitor came for

06

The main button is dead

"Commander" appears 3 times and is the primary CTA. On Tue 3 Nov at 16:13 it opened a Lightspeed "page not found", and it isn't on their Linktree either.

  1. Keep it as is
  2. Delete it
  3. One ORDER_URL setting. The buttons stay hidden until a working link is pasted there, and "Le menu" + "Itinéraire" become the primary actions. To confirm with the client
07

Opening hours appear nowhere on the site

They're only in a PDF behind Linktree.

  1. A table in the footer
  2. Live status from their hours (Europe/Zurich time): "Ouvert · jusqu'à 14:00" or "Fermé · ouvre à 18:00", shown in the nav, in the phone dock and as a neon sign that only lights when open. Full table with today marked, also sent to Google as structured data
08

The menu shows 4 dishes out of about 35

The TV menu PDF has wings 3/5/12/24, five burgers, Legends, Kids, sides, five sauces, drinks and the brookie.

  1. Link the PDF
  2. Embed an image of the board
  3. The full menu in HTML, the 8 boards as tabs (keyboard-accessible), plus a "Menu" switch that adds +5.50 / +8.50 exactly where the PDF says. The 3 Stars keep their own section
09

Their headline promise is buried

"GLUTEN FREE FRIED CHICKEN" is the first line of their Linktree bio. On the site it's a "GF" in a stats band.

  1. "Option gluten free" in the hero and on the fried-chicken step
10

Copy slips

"GRENNFIELD FESTIVAL 2026". The Paléo and Albanifest photos aren't in the festival list. "L'an dernier" will go out of date.

  1. Typo fixed (Greenfield), the rest kept verbatim and flagged below
11

The contact form only opens a mail app

It uses mailto:, which does nothing on a phone with no mail app set up.

  1. Formspree
  2. Netlify Forms (their current host, free tier, one attribute)
  3. For this staging copy: keep it and show info@pouletsecret.ch as a big link. On their domain: B
12

Staff tools are linked in the public footer

"Operations Center" and "Finance Center" are shown to every visitor, and both pages answer publicly.

  1. Removed from the public site. Recommend putting them behind a login or an unlinked URL

4 · Speed, search, sharing

13

The whole site is one 1.5 MB file

The 10 photos are inlined as base64 (98% of the bytes) and the logo is embedded twice. Nothing is cached separately and nothing lazy-loads. 1.14 MB transferred before anything shows.

  1. Separate WebP files, lazy below the fold, hero preloaded, logo as vector: about 344 KB gzipped for the first view, and every later visit is cached
14

Invisible to Google and to shares

No meta description, no share image, no favicon, no structured data, and robots.txt returns a 404.

  1. All added: Restaurant schema with address, hours and socials, a share image, icons. This staging copy is set to noindex on purpose

5 · Phones and accessibility

15

Phones get no navigation at all

Below 720 px the nav links disappear and nothing replaces them.

  1. Hamburger menu
  2. A bottom dock within thumb reach: live status, Le menu, Itinéraire
16

The slider moves on its own

It advances every 4 s with no pause, and the slides are background images with no alt text.

  1. You drive the gallery: it scrolls sideways on desktop and swipes on phones, using real images with alt text
17

Reduced-motion users

  1. With reduced motion on, the page drops all scroll effects and shows a complete static layout: the photo, every step, the full menu

6 · Look

18

The colours aren't theirs

The site is brown with gold neon. Their logo, menu screens and packaging are red, white, charcoal and a red/white checker.

  1. Their own colours: fryer black, logo red, charcoal from the menu screens, white tray paper with its checkered liner
19

It reads as a template

Anton + Work Sans + Space Mono labels, centred headings, bordered cards.

  1. Mona Sans Black, condensed (close to their menu-screen lettering), outlined/solid word pairs, no cards: lines and colour fields

To confirm with the client

  1. Is online ordering still live? If yes, send the working link and every "Commander" button comes back (one setting).
  2. Original, full-size photo files, especially the shop at night and the Cheesy Bacon. The site files are compressed to 900–1600 px.
  3. "N°1 fried chicken des festivals" and "5 ans": kept as they wrote them. Fine to keep as public claims?
  4. "L'an dernier, nous avons ouvert…": give the opening year so the sentence doesn't go out of date.
  5. The Secret du mois changes monthly. The drawn build matches the Cheesy Bacon and would be redrawn per edition (or swapped to a Star burger that stays on the menu).
